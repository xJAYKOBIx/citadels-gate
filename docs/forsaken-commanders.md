# Forsaken Commanders — character and art direction

Companion to `claude/covenant-commanders.md`: who the three Forsaken commanders are, how they talk, and how to get concept art made for them so the six read as one game. Stats stay in the Character Sheet; this doc is personality and look only.

## How the three fit together

The Forsaken are not an alliance; they are a hunger with three heads. Grom's Blood Legion is the wave that breaks the line, Ilsabet's dead hold the sky and the long memory, Mawgrom is the thing that walks up afterwards and knocks the walls down. None of them chose each other. Ilsabet believes she commands the other two, Grom believes nobody commands him, and Mawgrom was there before either of them and will be there after. Their barks should carry that friction: Grom finds Ilsabet's manners ridiculous and her drakes useful, Ilsabet finds Grom loud and Mawgrom slow and both of them necessary, Mawgrom mostly doesn't answer.

Shared visual rules so the three read as one faction and as the mirror of the Covenant: Forsaken crimson (#8e2630) as the dominant cloth, lacquer or war-paint colour, bone (#e3d6b8) for trim, teeth, horns and heraldry, black iron for armour, and the sickly green of the Bone Spire braziers (#a6f5c0) used sparingly as the only light source in the picture. Cool, desaturated skin for the living; no warm skin at all. Same framing as the Covenant: head-and-shoulders bust, three-quarter view, eyes toward the viewer, soft abstract background in the faction colours, no weapons raised. They have to read at 64 px on a card next to the Covenant three, so the silhouettes matter more than the detail.

## Warchief Grom — Leader of the Blood Legion

**In one line:** he has never lost a fight he was allowed to finish, and he intends to finish this one.

**Personality.** Grom is an orc in his prime, risen from nothing to warchief by the only ladder the Blood Legion has, and he is honest about it in a way the Covenant lords would find shocking. He counts kills out loud, not as a boast but as a tally: he wants to know what the day cost and what it bought. He is blunt, quick, physically restless, and funnier than people expect, with a dry contempt for anyone who hesitates. His flaw is tempo as a religion: he throws the next wave before the last has landed, because stopping feels like losing, and a patient enemy can bleed him on that. He despises Ilsabet's courtliness and trusts her drakes completely. He respects Mawgrom the way you respect a cliff. What he wants is simple and he says it: the keep, by sundown, and the Legion's name on it.

**Voice.** Deep, guttural, clipped, delivered at a half-shout even when calm. Numbers and short verbs. The occasional bark of laughter.

Deploy: "Go. Go. GO." / "Berserkers, ahead of me." / "Boar's up. Walk behind it."
Rank up: "More. Good. Send more."
Enemy elite sighted: "Big one. Means they're scared."
Strike called: "Meteor. Don't stand under it."
Tower lost: "One tower. Nine left. Move."
Winning: "The gate's open. I'll count them later."
Victory: "Twelve. No, thirteen. Thirteen, and the keep."
Defeat: "Fine. They bled for it. Next time they drown."

**Visual direction.** Massive neck and jaw, grey-green skin, lower tusks capped in bone, a shaved head with a single black topknot, three horizontal scars across the bridge of the nose, small dark eyes that are quick rather than dull. Black iron shoulder plate on one side only, the other shoulder bare and painted with a crimson handprint; a necklace of bone tallies (notched, lots of them); a crimson half-cloak of rough hide. Expression: the beginning of a grin with too many teeth. Silhouette cue: one huge pauldron, bare shoulder, topknot.

## Duchess Ilsabet Mourncroft — the Hollow Crown

**In one line:** she was a great lady once, and death has only improved her manners.

**Personality.** Ilsabet ruled a border duchy that the Covenant failed to defend; she made a bargain in the last winter of the siege and has been keeping her side of it ever since. She is cold, courtly, precise, and never raises her voice, because she no longer needs to breathe to speak. She addresses enemies with perfect etiquette and means every word of the threat underneath. Her flaw is that she cannot stop playing the long game: she holds her flyers back for the perfect moment and sometimes the moment is already gone, and she spends the dead as if they were endless, which they nearly are. She regards Grom as a useful animal and Mawgrom as weather. What she wants is the Covenant to admit, in her hearing, that they left her to die. The keep is merely how the conversation starts.

**Voice.** Quiet, dry, aristocratic, slightly hollow as if from the next room. Full sentences. Courtesy as a weapon.

Deploy: "Rise, and attend me." / "Shades. You know what to do." / "The Drake will join us presently."
Rank up: "The crown remembers. Open the next vault."
Enemy elite sighted: "How gauche. Kill it."
Strike called: "I have asked the sky for a small favour."
Tower lost: "Stone falls. We do not."
Winning: "Their gate is open. Do come in, all of you."
Victory: "You left me once. Note that I did not leave you."
Defeat: "Patience. I have had a great deal of practice at waiting."

**Visual direction.** Gaunt and still beautiful in the way of a cathedral: skin the colour of candle wax with the bones visible beneath, lips dark, eye sockets deep with a faint green ember in each, long black hair worn up in a formal court style gone slightly wrong. A tall crown of blackened silver with the top missing (the "hollow crown": a broken circlet with empty settings), a high-collared gown of crimson velvet faded to rust, bone-coloured lace at the throat, a chain of mourning jet. Expression: composed, faintly amused, chin lifted. Silhouette cue: the broken crown and the high collar.

## Mawgrom — the Mountain That Walks

**In one line:** he is older than the war, older than the forest, and he has decided to take an interest.

**Personality.** Mawgrom is a troll of the old kind, the sort that the hill-trolls tell stories about. He speaks slowly because he thinks slowly and thoroughly, and what he says is usually right and always too late to argue with. He is not cruel; he is indifferent in the way of landslides, and the trollkin who follow him love him for it, because he never asks them to be anything but trolls. His flaw is that he does not hurry, ever, and a quick enemy can be through his lines and gone before he has finished deciding. He has no opinion of Grom and a long one of Ilsabet, which he has never shared. What he wants is harder to say. Something about the stones remembering who moved them.

**Voice.** Enormous, slow, a landslide with grammar. Long pauses. Speaks of himself in the plural sometimes, meaning himself and the mountain.

Deploy: "Hurlers. Find a rock." / "Walk." / "We are coming."
Rank up: "Now the old ones wake."
Enemy elite sighted: "Small."
Strike called: "Stand away. The sky is heavy."
Tower lost: "Towers fall. Mountains do not notice."
Winning: "Their wall is thin. We can feel it."
Victory: "The stones will remember this. So will we."
Defeat: "We will be here. We are always here. Come back when you are ready."

**Visual direction.** A head like a boulder with a face in it: mossy grey-green hide (`#6b8a70`, the in-game troll skin), heavy brow ridge with lichen in the creases, small deep-set amber eyes, a wide flat nose, lower jaw with two short upward tusks, no hair but a crust of actual stone and moss across the crown and shoulders as if he has been sitting still for a century. A yoke of black iron and crimson-dyed rope across the shoulders with bone totems hanging from it, a cracked ram-skull pauldron. Expression: patient, almost gentle, which is worse. Silhouette cue: the stone crust on the skull and the yoke.

## Concept art: the same three directions

Use the direction you picked for the Covenant and nothing else; the six portraits sit side by side on the match-setup screen. The prompts are identical to the Covenant ones except for the shared suffix (colours swapped) and the character blocks.

**Shared suffix (append to every prompt):** head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter view, looking at the viewer, soft abstract background in deep crimson and bone, faction colours dark crimson and antique bone with a faint sickly green light, no text, no watermark, no weapon raised, centred composition with headroom.

### Direction A — painted fantasy portrait

`Oil painting portrait, fantasy realism, confident brushwork, cold green rim light against deep crimson shadow, textured canvas, [CHARACTER BLOCK], ` + shared suffix

### Direction B — graphic card art

`Stylised digital illustration, flat shading with hard-edged shadow shapes, limited palette, clean confident linework, slightly exaggerated features, painterly texture only in the background, [CHARACTER BLOCK], ` + shared suffix

### Direction C — illuminated manuscript

`Illuminated manuscript portrait, woodcut ink lines, tarnished silver leaf accents, scorched parchment texture, muted mineral pigments with dark crimson and bone, medieval illustration style with modern draughtsmanship, [CHARACTER BLOCK], ` + shared suffix

### Character blocks

**Grom:** `a massive orc warchief, grey-green skin, huge jaw with bone-capped lower tusks, shaved head with a single black topknot, three horizontal scars across the nose, small quick dark eyes, one black iron shoulder plate with the other shoulder bare and painted with a crimson handprint, a necklace of notched bone tallies, rough crimson hide half-cloak, expression a toothy beginning of a grin`

**Ilsabet:** `an undead noblewoman, gaunt and elegant, waxen pale skin with bone showing beneath, dark lips, deep eye sockets each with a faint green ember, long black hair in a formal court updo slightly undone, a broken crown of blackened silver with empty settings, a high-collared gown of faded crimson velvet with bone-coloured lace at the throat, a chain of black jet, expression composed and faintly amused with the chin lifted`

**Mawgrom:** `an ancient giant troll, head like a boulder, mossy grey-green hide, heavy brow ridge with lichen in the creases, small deep-set amber eyes, wide flat nose, two short upward tusks from the lower jaw, a crust of stone and moss across the skull and shoulders, a yoke of black iron and crimson rope hung with bone totems, a cracked ram-skull shoulder guard, expression patient and almost gentle`

### Practical notes

Same as the Covenant: 1:1 for the cards, 2:3 for the setup panel, four of each, keep the one whose face survives being shrunk to a thumbnail. Check the six together before settling: Grom and Borvik are both broad and grinning, so make sure Grom's is a threat and Borvik's is a joke; Ilsabet and Kyndrili are both pale and long-faced, so the broken crown and the feather mantle have to do the work; Mawgrom should be the only one of the six whose head nearly fills the frame.

When the portraits exist, the cards swap from the crests to `art/portraits/<commander>.png` (512×512) and the match-setup panel to the 2:3 crops; the Library's commander pages can take the barks as flavour text under the title.
