Citadel's Gate

Lead one side of a winter war across a frozen river. Wave infantry march the two outer lanes on their own; the middle lane is yours to fill with gold. You spend gold on everything else, draw their paths, seize the high ground, and break both towers in any lane to expose the enemy stronghold.

How to play
Deploy
Tap a card in the Grimoire, then tap the map (or drag the card there). Every unit marches out of your keep's gate (flyers lift off the keep) at double speed until it clears the blue circle around your stronghold. Drop inside the circle and it goes to that spot; anywhere else: fighters dropped on a lane march that lane, casters, caravans and anti-air walk to the spot and hold it, and flyers fly there and circle.
Command
Press on one of your units and drag to draw its path. Release to send it. Close the loop to make it patrol. End on an enemy to attack it.
Flyers
Flyers never stop. Draw their path across enemy ground to strafe or breathe fire along it; without orders they circle and chase nearby flyers.
Lanes
Wave infantry march the two outer lanes on their own; the middle lane only gets what you buy. Fighting units dropped on a lane march it automatically, and so does any unit you walk back onto a lane. Units left off the lane hold their ground until you give them a path.
Gold
Gold fills on its own up to 30 and stops when full, so keep spending. Relic caches appear on the rune circles; move a ground unit onto one for 10–25 gold.
Ranks
You start at Rank I with the scout and the front line. Break the Rank II seal (12 gold) for spawners, flyers and casters, then the Rank III seal (25 gold) and the Rank IV seal (28 gold) for much stronger units. You can field up to 4 of each Rank I–II unit, 2 of each Rank III and 1 of each Rank IV at a time.
High ground
Units on higher ground shoot 20% farther and hit 25% harder, and units below can't target them unless they come close. Casters and flyers ignore height. On Frostmere the hills are climbed by their ramps. On Thornspire Pass bare rock can't be climbed at all: roads and stairs are the only ways up, and the stairs lead to the Hawk Ridge and Wolf Ledge lookouts.
Bridges and tunnels
On Thornspire Pass, Kingsbridge carries the middle road high over the gorge, and a tunnel through Greyspine links the north saddle with Hawk Ridge. Units in a tunnel can't be seen or hit until they come out. The gorge floor is a hidden path between the two low crossings, with a stair up to the middle road. Draw a path across any of them and your unit will use it.
Reading the field
Zoom out and every fighting unit shows a badge with its portrait, a ring in its side's colour and a health ring when hurt. Gold pips mark Rank III and IV units.
Power
Your commander's strike (24 gold, 60 s cooldown) hits anywhere on the map. Tap it, then tap a target.
Camera
On a phone or tablet, hold it sideways like a controller: the view always faces the enemy stronghold, drag to pan and pinch to zoom. Zooming out lifts the camera high over the whole field; zooming in drops it down among the troops. Double-tap to switch between the whole field and a close view, and tap the minimap to jump. With a mouse: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag to turn and tilt (or Q/E and Z/X), C to reset.

Counters matter: flyers shred infantry and caravans, anti-air shreds flyers, casters punish clumps from out of tower range, scouts kill unguarded casters, cannons and beetles kill armor.
